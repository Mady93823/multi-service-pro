# For service professionals

## Getting approved

1. Sign up and choose **provider**.
2. Fill in your profile: what you do, your experience, the categories you work in, your working hours, where you are based, and how far you will travel.
3. Upload your documents (ID, and anything else the platform asks for).
4. Wait for the team to review them.

If something is rejected, you will be told why, and you can replace it and resubmit. **You can open a support ticket at any time, including before you are approved** — if you are stuck in review, that is exactly when you need help most.

## Getting jobs

- You only receive job offers when you are **online**. The toggle is on your dashboard.
- Offers go to the nearest available professionals in the right category, inside their travel radius. **An offer expires** — if you do not answer, it moves to someone else.
- **Blackouts** are days you mark as unavailable. You will not be offered anything on them.

## Doing a job

The buttons on the job screen follow the visit, in order:

1. **Start journey** — this turns on live tracking. Keep the screen on; the customer is watching you move on a map.
2. **Arrived** — you are at the door.
3. **Start job** — the customer reads you a short code from their app and you type it in. This is how the platform knows the job really started, and it protects both of you.
4. **Complete** — the job is done.

If the customer is paying **cash**, you collect the full amount at the door — including the platform's commission and the tax. That amount is then deducted from what the platform owes you, which is why a cash-only week can leave your balance negative. It settles itself against your next online jobs.

## Live tracking

- Your phone shares its location only **during** a job, from *Start journey* until you arrive. Never before, never after.
- It needs location permission and an HTTPS connection. If you refuse permission, the screen tells you how to turn it back on.
- **A dropped connection never loses a job.** Your position is saved first and broadcast second — if the network hiccups, the customer's map catches up.

## Getting paid

- **Earnings** shows every job, what the platform took, and what is yours.
- Money becomes claimable after a holding period.
- Add a **payout account** (UPI or bank), then request a payout. A request claims your whole released balance. You can have one open request at a time.
- Editing a payout account after a payout has been paid **does not change what that payout says it paid to** — the record is a snapshot, on purpose.

## Your rating

Customers rate you after a completed job. Your average is **recalculated** from the reviews that are currently visible — so a review the team removes really does leave your average.
