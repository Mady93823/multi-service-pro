# For customers

Everything you need to book a service, watch your professional arrive, pay, and share how it went. You can do all of it from a phone browser — and you can add the site to your home screen so it opens like an app.

## Creating your account

- Sign up with your phone number or email. If the site asks for a one-time code, it is sent to your phone.
- Add your **address** with a pin on the map — this is where the professional comes, so drop it accurately.
- You can save more than one address (home, office, a parent's place) and pick the right one at checkout.

## Finding a service

1. Browse by category, or search for what you need.
2. Open a service to see what it includes, how long it takes, the price, and any optional **add-ons**.
3. Some services are **fixed price**, some are **hourly**, and some are **"inspection first"** — for those, a professional assesses the job on site and gives you the final quote before any work starts.
4. Planning an event? The **Events** page has wedding, birthday and party services, booked the same easy way.

> If a service is not available at your address, it is because it is outside the areas currently served. Try another saved address, or check back as coverage grows.

## Booking

1. Add the service (and any add-ons) to your cart. You can book more than one service in a single visit.
2. Choose a **date and time slot**. The slots you see are in your city's local time.
3. Choose the **address** and give a **contact phone** the professional can reach you on.
4. Apply a **coupon** if you have one.
5. Choose how to pay.

### Ways to pay

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **UPI / card / netbanking / wallet** | You pay online now. Your booking is confirmed the moment the payment succeeds. |
| **Your wallet** | If you have a balance (from a refund or a referral reward), you can spend it here. |
| **Bank transfer** | Transfer to the listed account and upload your receipt; your booking is confirmed once the team verifies it. |
| **Pay after service** | Available in many areas — pay the professional in cash or by UPI when the job is done. Some areas are online-only. |

If you choose an online method and do not complete payment, the booking simply expires on its own — nothing is charged and no professional is sent out.

## Watching your professional arrive

Once a professional accepts your booking and sets off, open the booking to see them **live on a map**.

- The marker moves in real time and shows an **estimated arrival**.
- Tracking is on only while they are travelling to you — not before, not after.
- If your connection wobbles, the map catches up on its own.

When they arrive, they will ask you for a short **code shown on your booking**. Read it out — it confirms the right professional is at your door before work begins.

## After the visit

- Your **invoice** is available as a GST PDF from the booking page.
- Pay any outstanding cash or UPI amount directly to the professional if you chose pay-after-service.

## Rating your professional

After a completed job you can leave a **review**: a star rating, a few words, and photos if you like. Your honest rating helps other customers and keeps standards high.

## Your wallet

- Refunds are credited to your **wallet**, ready to spend on your next booking.
- **Refer a friend**: share your referral code from the wallet page. When someone you invite completes their first booking, you are rewarded.

## Rebooking and favourites

- **Book again** from any past booking to pre-fill your cart with the same service.
- **Favourite** a professional you liked, so future bookings can try them first.

## Managing a booking

- **Reschedule** or **cancel** from the booking page, within the windows the platform allows. A cancellation made late may carry a small fee, which is shown to you before you confirm.
- Every booking keeps a clear status — placed, on the way, in progress, completed — so you always know where things stand.

## Getting help

- **Help** opens a support ticket — from a specific booking, or on its own. You will be notified when the team replies.
- Not signed in? The **Contact** page on the site reaches the same team.
